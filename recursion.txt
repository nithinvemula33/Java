---Recursion---
def : when a function calls itself until a specific condition met. 
There are two types of recursions
-->parameterised 
-->functional

**Parameterised**
-->Uses extra parameters to carry the answer.
-->Base case usually prints or returns the accumulated result.
-->Answer builts before the recursive call.
-->Easier for problems where you keep updating a result.

**Functional**
-->Returns the answer from each recursive call.
-->Base case returns a simple value.
-->Answer builts after the recursive call returns.
-->Used for problems where results are built from subproblems.
